SELECTED WORK

Two research problems, shown at a high level.

01 RHEED / THIN-FILM GROWTH Rutgers University
Topological Quantum Matter Engineering Lab August 2026 — Present

Seeing more in a grazing-angle measurement.

Problem
RHEED is invaluable during thin-film growth, but a single measurement can make reciprocal-space structure difficult to interpret.
Goal
Explore a clearer way to reconstruct and visualize structural information so evolving growth patterns can be inspected more directly.
02 NEUTRAL-ATOM SYSTEMS / ML-ASSISTED CONTROL Rice University
Jo Lab May 25, 2026 — July 31, 2026

Toward Atom Rearrangement.

Problem
Neutral-atom rearrangement requires programmable optical spot arrays that can be generated reliably across many target geometries—not only a single 8×8 pattern.
Goal
Use machine learning to control a spatial light modulator (SLM), generating and evaluating diverse camera-recorded optical spot arrays as a step toward future atom-rearrangement experiments.

ABOUT

My interests center on scalable and compact neutral-atom quantum platforms. I am particularly interested in scaling neutral-atom arrays to larger atom numbers, reducing the footprint and complexity of neutral-atom quantum computing systems, and using programmable neutral-atom platforms for quantum simulation and other experiments.
